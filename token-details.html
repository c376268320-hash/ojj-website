Project and implemented functionality

OhJingJing (OJJ) was initiated under the public name OJJOJJ. The deliverables described here are a deployed fixed-supply token contract on BNB Smart Chain and its public project information.

The contract implements balance queries, transfers and spending allowances. It does not implement staking yield, lending, voting governance, dividends, a buyback mechanism or a guaranteed price. Holding OJJ does not automatically grant these features through this contract.

This is a technical fact sheet, not a fundraising page, business plan, security audit or promise of future products.

Contract controls and verification

  • Non-upgradeable ERC-20 contract with a one-time supply minted in its constructor.
  • No administrator, public additional mint function, transfer tax or blacklist functionality.
  • Token spending approvals are ordinary ERC-20 allowances. Granting an allowance to another contract can expose the approved tokens to risk.
  • Sourcify reported exact matches for creation and runtime code. BscScan source-code exact-match status was checked on 2026-09-22.
  • No independent security audit has been performed. Source-code matching does not certify security or confer an explorer endorsement.

Risks and limitations

The initial allocation is concentrated in one address. Concentration and subsequent transfers or sales can pose material risks.

A fixed supply does not establish a market price, market demand, liquidity or redeemable value. No returns, buybacks or exchange listings are promised. Participants can lose the full value of funds committed.

Blockchain transactions may be irreversible. Wallet compromise, incorrect addresses, malicious approvals and smart-contract vulnerabilities can cause losses.
